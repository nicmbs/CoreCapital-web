## 2. What CoreCapital is, for the purposes of this Policy

CoreCapital is a **software tool** that lets you record, consolidate and view information about investments you declare yourself, and obtain analysis of that information generated by artificial-intelligence assistants.

**CoreCapital does not hold users' funds or assets, does not process payments, does not execute financial transactions and takes no part in the purchase or sale of any instrument.** The information we process is, for the most part, information you enter yourself.

We process your data in accordance with this Policy and with the data protection law applicable in your place of residence.

## 3. Guiding principle: we deliberately collect little

Before the detail, the essentials:

- **We do not ask for your identity document number, your address or your date of birth.** There is no identity-document upload in the Platform.
- **We do not access your bank or broker accounts.** Your portfolio information is what **you declare**.
- **We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.**
- **We do not use your information to train artificial-intelligence models.**

This data is sent directly from your browser to our database, using a public access key that **only permits creating records**: it does not permit reading, modifying or deleting them.

## 6. Automated decisions and profiling

The Platform **does build a profile** of you in a meaningful sense: it combines your declared investor profile, your reference currency and your portfolio composition to **automatically generate analyses and summaries** using language models.

On this we are explicit:

1. **None of these outputs produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you.** They are informational content displayed on screen. We **do not make automated decisions** about your access to the service, your price or your creditworthiness.
2. **No automated output constitutes advice** or a personalised investment recommendation.
3. **You may object.** You may request that we disable automatic analysis generation and assistant memory, while retaining all other features. Write to the address in section 1.
4. **You may report an error to us.** If an automated analysis looks wrong to you, you can tell us at the address in section 1.

The only automated decisions with a **concrete operational effect** are technical: **applying your plan's usage limits** and the **automatic anti-fraud and rate-limiting controls**, which may temporarily block an action. **In those cases you may request review by a member of our team, put your point of view and contest the outcome**, by writing to the address in section 1.

## 7. Artificial intelligence: what is sent, and to whom

We include this section in detail because **your portfolio data does leave our systems** and reaches model providers.

### 7.1 What is transmitted

When you use an assistant, the following is transmitted to the model provider:

- the **text of your query**;
- the assistant's **system instructions**;
- depending on the assistant, **your portfolio data** obtained from an internal function that returns only your own active investments. The fields actually transmitted are: investment identifier, asset name, asset type and class, currency, nominal annual rate, start and closing dates, and — **importantly** — **the capital invested and capital contributed**, that is, **the amounts of your positions**. Your **investor profile and reference currency** are also transmitted;
- the **relevant conversation history** and, in the assistant that has it enabled, your **preference memory**.

### 7.2 What is NOT transmitted

Your credentials, your authentication factors and other users' data are **not** transmitted to model providers. The support assistant additionally **has no access to your real portfolio data**.

### 7.3 Who the providers are

The assistants rely on large language models supplied by third parties and orchestrated by our platform provider:

| Assistant | Model provider | Processing location |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio Assistant | **Anthropic, PBC** (United States) | United States |
| Portfolio analysis · Investment recording · Support | **Google LLC** (United States) | United States |

Orchestration, application hosting and product analytics are carried out through **Base44 Ltd.**, our platform-as-a-service provider.

> **Why we name them.** The product's internal policy avoids showing provider names in the interface. **That rule does not apply to this document**: identifying who receives your personal data is a transparency obligation and prevails over any branding preference.

### 7.4 Commitments

- **Your data is not used to train these providers' models.** We engage them on enterprise terms that exclude training on the data transmitted.
- **We do not use your data to train our own models.**
- Providers may **temporarily retain** the transmitted data for security and abuse-prevention purposes, for limited periods defined in their own policies.
- They act as **processors** on our behalf and under documented instructions.

### 7.5 Your choice

Use of the AI assistants is **voluntary**. You can record, consolidate and view your portfolio without interacting with them. If you prefer your data not to be transmitted to model providers, simply do not use those features and request that automatic analysis generation be disabled (section 6, point 3).

## 11. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including:

- **encryption in transit** (TLS) for all communications;
- **per-user access control at the database level**, so that each user can only read and write their own records;
- **optional strong authentication** via a second factor and passkeys bound to your device;
- **minimisation**: the function that supplies data to the AI assistant returns only the authenticated user's data and a limited subset of fields;
- **versioned records** of legal acceptances, with date and time.

No system is completely secure. We recommend enabling the second factor, not reusing credentials, and signing out on shared devices.

### 11.1 Security incidents

If a personal data breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights, we will **notify you without undue delay**, describing its nature, the categories affected, the likely consequences and the measures taken, and we will report it to the relevant supervisory authority.

**Where applicable law sets a different deadline, threshold or procedure, we will comply with what that law requires.**
